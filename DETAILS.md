Mission
To partner with communities in Kajiado County to strengthen lasting climate resilience, foster sustainable livelihoods, and empower people with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to guide their own development and create a better future for their families and communities. 
Vision
A climate-resilient Kajiado County where empowered communities create sustainable livelihoods and inclusive economic opportunities for lasting prosperity.
About Us
INUKA 34 is a community-centered organization working alongside people and communities in Kajiado County to build resilient, self-reliant, and prosperous futures.
We believe communities already hold the strength, knowledge, and creativity needed to shape their own futures when they have access to the right opportunities, skills, support, and resources. That is why we partner with communities, rather than simply working for them, to identify local challenges, co-create practical solutions, and drive lasting change.
Kajiado County has tremendous potential, yet many communities continue to face the effects of climate change, environmental degradation, food insecurity, limited economic opportunities, and changing livelihoods. INUKA 34 responds by linking climate action with economic empowerment and community development to help communities thrive and build long-term resilience.
Our work is guided by four interconnected areas: climate change interventions, sustainable agroecology, economic empowerment, and holistic community development.
•	
We invest in people
•	

We believe lasting change begins with knowledge, skills, and empowered people. Through training, mentorship, and practical learning, we help individuals and communities build the skills they need to improve their livelihoods and take greater responsibility for their own development.
Our training responds to community needs and covers sustainable agriculture, agroecology, environmental conservation, climate resilience, entrepreneurship, green skills, livelihood development, and community leadership. We value practical learning that people can apply in their homes, farms, businesses, and communities.
We also recognize that people face challenges that cannot be addressed through economic opportunities or environmental interventions alone. Counseling and psychosocial support are part of our holistic approach to community development. We provide safe, supportive spaces where people can receive guidance, build confidence, cope with difficult circumstances, strengthen relationships, and make informed choices about their lives and futures.

•	
We connect people and the environment
•	

We believe a healthy environment should create opportunities for people. Trees can provide food, shade, income, biodiversity, and protection for the land. Community gardens can improve nutrition while creating income opportunities. Sustainable farming practices can protect the soil while helping households produce more food.
This is why we combine environmental action with food security, skills development, economic empowerment, and community wellbeing. Our work is rooted in the belief that environmental sustainability and human wellbeing go hand in hand, and that lasting change happens when communities are empowered to thrive.

•	
We believe that practical innovation can create lasting change. 
•	

Solutions do not need to be expensive or complicated to make a meaningful difference. We champion the use of local knowledge, creativity, and practical innovation to help communities address their own challenges, strengthen ownership, and take pride in solutions they can sustain.
 
This includes sustainable farming, agroforestry, water-efficient growing systems, community gardens, and the productive reuse of materials that would otherwise become waste. We seek solutions that communities can understand, afford, own, and sustain—solutions that build resilience, dignity, and long-term impact.

•	
We place communities at the center of everything we do
•	

We work alongside women, teenagers, youth, girls, pastoralist families, and other communities facing vulnerability to create meaningful opportunities for learning, participation, leadership, support, livelihoods, and contribution to solutions that shape their futures.
We do not believe in simply delivering solutions to communities. Instead, we partner closely with local leaders and community members to identify, develop, and sustain culturally appropriate, community-owned solutions that reflect local priorities and drive lasting change.

•	
We see the connections
•	

We see environmental degradation, climate change, food insecurity, unemployment, poverty, and social challenges as deeply interconnected. So too are the solutions. This is why our work inspires collective responsibility, shared action, and hope for lasting change.
 
A young person can receive training, build a green enterprise, and create employment while contributing to environmental conservation. A farmer can learn agroecological practices, improve food production, protect the soil, and increase household income. A community can restore degraded land while creating opportunities for local participation, resilience, and dignified livelihoods.
 
This is the kind of transformation INUKA 34 exists to advance.

Our Values
Environmental Stewardship
We believe that protecting and restoring the environment is a shared responsibility. We are committed to caring for our land, forests, water, and other natural resources and encouraging communities to become responsible stewards of their environment.
Community Empowerment
We believe lasting change happens when communities are involved in creating solutions to their own challenges. We work to equip individuals and communities with the knowledge, skills, resources, and opportunities they need to take action and become agents of change.
Sustainability
We focus on solutions that create lasting environmental, social, and economic benefits. Our goal is not simply to plant trees today, but to nurture initiatives that continue benefiting communities and the environment for generations.
Inclusivity
We believe everyone should have an opportunity to participate in and benefit from development. We particularly seek to involve youth, women, girls, and vulnerable communities in our environmental and economic initiatives.
Integrity
We are committed to honesty, transparency, accountability, and responsible use of resources. We strive to build trust with the communities, partners, and individuals we work with.
Collaboration
We believe meaningful environmental and social change requires people to work together. We seek partnerships with communities, schools, organizations, businesses, and other stakeholders to multiply our impact.
Innovation
We believe environmental solutions do not have to be expensive or complicated to be effective. We encourage practical creativity by exploring methods such as specialized pipes and other water-efficient systems for growing plants, as well as repurposing plastic bottles and other discarded materials into planting and growing solutions. By combining local knowledge with creative approaches, we aim to make conservation and sustainable food production more accessible while tackling waste.
 What Makes Us Different
We are not simply a tree-planting organization. We are building an ecosystem of change around every seed we plant.
Our approach connects climate action, forest restoration, food security, waste reduction, innovation, employment, and community empowerment rather than treating these as separate problems. We look for solutions that can address several challenges at once—for example, reusing plastic waste as a growing resource, developing water-efficient planting systems, growing food-producing plants, and creating opportunities for young people and communities to participate in and benefit from these initiatives.
We don't just plant trees. We plant opportunities. We plant food. We plant skills. We plant livelihoods. And ultimately, we plant the foundation for a more resilient community.
